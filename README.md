# Saturday 21 November 2026 — The World in One Day

An entry for make.fun's **news-image-20261121** competition
([bounty page](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261121)): one image that
sums up the world's news of Saturday 21 November 2026.

The image is a broadsheet front page: a masthead naming the day, a
by-the-numbers strip, a colour region bar, 13 illustrated stories,
23 further briefs and a DATE CHECK box — 36 stories across 6 regions
in total.

Hero stories: Iran's strikes on US bases in Bahrain, the Russian missile
that hit Kryvyi Rih (Zelensky's home town), and Wall Street's AI sell-off
into correction. Also drawn: Mexico's capture of FBI most-wanted cartel
boss "Chapo Isidro", the EU–Indonesia trade deal signed in Brussels,
COP31's fossil-fuel deadlock in Antalya, two tankers hit near Hormuz,
and the Shenzhen summit fallout.

## Files

- `entry.png` — the entry image (1024×1024, generated by `render.py`).
- `render.py` — the generator: researched stories, layout and all artwork,
  drawn with PIL primitives in Liberation fonts. Deterministic output.
- `main.py` — the competition program (Python 3.13, standard library
  only): copies `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png`, no network.

## Run

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

## Sources

Stories were cross-checked across OneTurn's 21 November 2026 front page,
Al Jazeera, BBC News, UPI, France 24, The Hindu, UKMTO reporting and
BleepingComputer. Only stories dated Saturday 21 November 2026 are
included; the DATE CHECK box on the page records what was deliberately
left out (Friday's Poland missile fall and Cyclone Akvan landfall,
Sunday's Peru quake wires).
